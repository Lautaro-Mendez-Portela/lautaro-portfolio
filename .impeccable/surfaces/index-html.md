## Scope and mode

Stages 1 through 4 for the root portfolio surface. Visitor mode: Persuade, with the offer, proof, process, author and contact path presented cinematically rather than as a technical CV.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Non-technical business clients must understand the offer, inspect proof and process, recognize the author, and reach an honest contact close; recruiters are secondary. The supplied `1080p.mp4` remains the only final visual asset. No contact or social URL may be invented: unavailable destinations stay visibly pending. No Stage 5 audit, global polish, invented claim, extra image, heavy dependency, parallax, scroll zoom, WebGL, or 3D is allowed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A cinematic client proposal resolves into a clear working relationship: proof gives way to process, person and invitation; it refuses the generic four-card timeline, oversized biography card and contact form template.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black fields, off-white type, a restrained cyan signal, hairline dividers, open editorial columns and generous negative space. The process is drawn as one connected line, personal facts remain typographic, and pending contact destinations are explicit states rather than fake links.

STORY: After the offer and project proof, the visitor understands the four-step collaboration, meets the developer in a quieter personal pause, and reaches a decisive closing invitation. WhatsApp, email and social destinations remain prepared but truthfully pending until real data exists.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The approved Hero remains untouched. Stage 4 begins after Proyectos with a compact left-aligned title/subtitle group and a single four-step timeline. A quiet base rail is progressively covered in cyan as each stage activates; mobile translates the same sequence into one continuous vertical axis before the page quiets into Sobre mí and expands again for Contacto.

FORM: Local extension of grounded direction 4, cinematic client-proposal title card, seed `fb07d1ed`. The close inherits poster-scale conviction, negative-space discipline, fine rules and continuous-black pacing; no new visual direction is introduced.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unresolved decisions

Real WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn, GitHub and project-detail destinations remain intentionally unresolved. Definitive service/project captures also remain pending. Contact controls and footer destinations expose that state visibly and are non-navigable until configured. The static HTML/CSS/JS stack remains sufficient and dependency-free.
